# Quiet Lift

Movement begins with a grounded curve and ends in open air. A forward-leaning silhouette carries the weight; a small, rising wing releases it. Meticulously crafted curves should feel continuous even where the shapes separate. The space between them is part of the drawing.

One warm orange gives the form its energy. Flat color, transparent counters and an open background keep the mark clear on both light and dark surfaces. Its identity comes from the silhouette, so a single-color reproduction retains the same character.

Scale is a design constraint. The bowl stays open, the stem remains substantial, and the upper wing has enough separation to survive a browser tab. Painstaking attention to the small silhouette matters as much as the large presentation; ornament must earn its pixels.

Composition balances the rounded lower mass against the rising upper edge. Generous space and restrained typography let that tension carry the identity. Motion, when present, is a brief lift in response to interaction, with the static drawing carrying the full idea for people who prefer reduced motion.
